## 2. Predictive Capabilities

### 1. 30-Day Demand Forecasting

- Analyzes multi-month historical site consumption using weighted moving averages.
- Calculates projected daily consumption rates.
- Suggests optimal replenishment quantities to prevent site run-out while respecting working capital limits.

### 2. Abnormal Purchase Price Alerts

- Flags incoming quotations or proposed PO rates that deviate $> 10\%$ above the 90-day moving average benchmark.
- Prevents price gouging or unauthorized rate revisions during high-demand commodity cycles.

### 3. Material Consumption Anomaly Detection

- Compares real-time daily consumption against engineering standards (Bill of Quantities / BOQ).
- Flags surges $> 35\%$ over standard consumption rates to investigate site leakage, double-batch casting, or inaccurate measurements.

### 4. Project Cost-to-Complete Forecasting

- Combines historical burn rate with remaining scheduled work packages to project Estimate-at-Completion (EAC) and flag projected cost overruns before they occur.
